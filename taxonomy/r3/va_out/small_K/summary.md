# R3 pass small_K: smaller Architecture leaves (K1–K12) and gardens and plant arts (K13–K14)
(saved by the coordinator from the pass report)

## What changed
- **Complete buildings catalogue for every style leaf** from architecture_exemplars.csv and architecture_nonwestern_exemplars.csv (for gardens, tulou, hórreos, trulli, kominka and ksour, by the building type in the data). One rule everywhere: every gathered building or site with a proper Latin-, Greek- or Cyrillic-script name and a Wikipedia article or a UNESCO/national heritage listing. Source = its Wikidata page; gloss = type, place, country, date, UNESCO/heritage status.
- **Sections follow the field's own axis**: by dynasty or phase (Bahri/Burji Mamluk; Umayyad/Abbasid at 750; Ottoman by century; Khmer pre-Angkorian/Angkorian/provincial; Pagan/Mrauk U; Sukhothai/Lanna/Ayutthaya/Rattanakosin; Japanese temples Asuka to Edo; Chinese Tang to Qing; Korean Three Kingdoms/Goryeo/Joseon); by sub-style (Bengal chala/ratna, Shinto honden styles, kofun shapes, Romanesque regional schools); by culture (Mesoamerican and Andean). Large sections split by region, district or building type; rows in date order.
- **Tightened where the rule gives > 400 rows**: ≥ 3 articles or UNESCO for Nagara, Kerala, Tibetan Buddhist, Thai; ≥ 5 for Dravidian, Chinese, Shinto shrines, Japanese castles, Kofun; ≥ 8 for Japanese Buddhist.
- **Wikidata search under the same rule for thin leaves**: Moldavian churches, stepwells, havelis, tulou, hanok, wharenui, marae, Chiloé churches, hórreos, trulli, the seven garden types.
- **2–5 View-all catalogues per leaf**: a Wikidata query for every building tagged with the style; Archnet, Islamic Architectural Heritage, Discover Islamic Art for Islamic leaves; UNESCO searches, NRHP, SAH Archipedia, Historic England, POP/Mérimée, Rijksmonumentenregister, ASI, Japanese and Korean heritage databases, Pleiades, CRSBI, archINFORM, Docomomo, Parks & Gardens UK, checked Wikipedia lists.
- Sub-styles, sub-types and centres first (machine bucket rows renamed to attested sub-style names); architects last, by birth, glosses from architecture_architects.csv; gardens use Makers; Possession rows kept with note and URL.
