## Doubts and limits
- About 73% of the bucket is still residue. These are mostly regional academic, landscape and portrait painters with no movement evidence in the data. I moved nobody on a guess, so many are left under their country.
- Judgement calls: Fritz Erler and the Die Scholle painters were left out of Nazi art. Ivo Saliger and Paul Mathias Padua were put in it. Capogrossi went to Informalism, not Scuola Romana. Moved Scottish and Glasgow Style designers (Mackintosh to Art Nouveau Architecture). Jacob Epstein went to Modern Sculpture, not Vorticism. Daniel Buren is in the BMPT section, not Conceptual Art.
- Figuration narrative (7) and the Group of Seven (3) are residue sections, not leaves: the first has no English Wikipedia article and the second has too few members here. Leipzig School has only 7 members in the data.
- Countries come from the first Wikidata country. Mixed entries (Austria–Hungary, Kingdom of Serbs, Russian Empire, Ottoman Empire) were sorted by hand. A few are still probably in the wrong country section.
- Not Western Europe but in this input: Soviet republics, the Balkans, the Americas, the British Raj and the Dutch East Indies. These are grouped by country in the same leaf.
