## Doubts
- 143 adds carry no id: the pink, Roman Porno, Heimatfilm, chanchada, rubble-film and Korean New Wave canons are mostly absent from the pools. The titles and years come from the film histories but still need ids.
- Cycle and movement leaves take some films out of their genre homes, e.g. The Breakfast Club, Y tu mamá también, Tombs of the Blind Dead and Baby Face. Only films emblematic of the cycle were moved; genre anchors such as Mad Max and Amores perros stayed.
- Comedia Ranchera has no dedicated en.wiki article; it is sourced to Golden Age of Mexican Cinema and Allá en el Rancho Grande.
- Duplicate leaves remain untouched: A12-4 and A13-10 (Screwball), A3-22 and A12-50 (Spy Comedy), A5-14 and A6-21 (Lost World).
