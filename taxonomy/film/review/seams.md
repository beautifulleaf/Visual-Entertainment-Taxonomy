# Seams and leaf gaps reported by the film reviewers

Fixed during the run (leaves or precedence changed before later batches):
- Partisan Film broadened to Albanian and other Eastern-bloc partisan films (batch 01).
- F20: transposed westerns include other frontiers (Australian bushranger films, *Aferim!*) (batches 01, 03).
- F21 added: genre films merely set in wartime go by their genre (*Sisu*) (batch 11).
- A18-1 Masala Film covers Bangladeshi (Dhallywood) and Pakistani (Lollywood) masala (batch 02).
- New leaves: A2-12 Cine Quinqui (batch 11), A1-35 Cinema Marginal (batch 03).

Open (for the owner):
- Ozploitation has no leaf; Australian genre films linked from the Australian New Wave article are placed by genre (batch 01).
- Long multi-genre narrative experiments (*La Flor*, *Trenque Lauquen*) and docu-fiction hybrids (*Cold Tropics*, *Ten Canoes*) fit no leaf well (batches 01, 03).
- Hood film is defined as American; *City of God* stays Crime Drama with Hood Film as see-also (batch 03).
- Fantasy martial-arts films (*Mortal Kombat*) go to Fantasy by key order (batch 02).
- Kaurismäki's deadpan films; Akerman's minimalist features (A14-5 / A16-1 / A1-33) (batches 02, 11).
- Real single-case films (*I Am Nevenka*, *Difret*): biopic vs legal vs social drama (batch 11).
- Web-series finale released in cinemas (*The Amazing Digital Circus: The Last Act*) (batch 02).
- F22 added after batches 05, 09, 10 (crime comedy unreachable by key order; parody westerns; sports biopics).
- F23 added after batches 05, 08 (are vampire/zombie leaves cycles for D1? no; remakes of cycle films go by genre).
- Open: no leaves for Edgar Wallace krimi, Trümmerfilm, killer-children horror, women-in-prison outside Toei, anthology horror, 2D digital (non-3D) animation, superpowers without a heroic identity, late Godard essay-fictions, post-war aftermath films.
