# Coverage pass: animation (family C, C1–C14)

Built by `build.py` (helper lookup: `q.py`). Totals: 4 new leaves, 4 new sections, 5 resections, 40 moves, 414 adds (one has no id).

## Audit
- **Anime, against MAL genres, themes and demographics.** Most terms already have a leaf or section: isekai, iyashikei, CGDCT, mecha (super/real robot, Gundam), magical girl, idol, music, gourmet, workplace, childcare/family, sports by sport, mind sports, BL, yuri, villainess, delinquents, samurai, vampire, time travel, video game, detective, medical, military, mythology, showbiz/performing arts, psychological, high-stakes game, organized crime, avant-garde (C8-10), kodomo, sekai-kei and mecha musume.
- **Anime, missing:**
  - MAL's *Crossdressing* and *Magical Sex Shift* themes (the old "Gender Bender" genre) were missing. Added NEW3 **Gender-Bender Anime** and moved in Ranma ½, Kämpfer, Stop!! Hibari-kun!, Otoboku, Maria†Holic, Onimai and others.
  - The Shōwa **spokon** cycle was split across the sport leaves. Added NEW2 **Spokon**: Kyojin no Hoshi, Attack No.1, Ashita no Joe, Ace wo Nerae!, Tiger Mask and more.
- **Fundamentals (era, school, studio):**
  - C11-5 Early Japanese Animation is re-sectioned by era (Meiji–Taishō / early Shōwa and wartime / postwar to early Tōei Dōga). Added Tōei's first features and Ōfuji's Kujira.
  - C8-10 Art Anime is re-sectioned by school: Mushi Pro experiments and Animerama (Senya Ichiya, Cleopatra and Belladonna together), festival shorts (Tezuka, Yamamura, Kick-Heart), and auteur features.
  - C1-8 Seinen Action now has an "OVA-Boom" era section. Other unplaced OVA-boom titles (Gall Force, Zeorymer, Dangaiou, Kishin Heidan, Violence Jack 3) went to their genre leaves.
- **Western and international, missing or thin:**
  - NEW4 **Soviet Animation** (78 titles; sections follow Soyuzmultfilm's own history: Socialist-Realist fairy tales / the 1960s "aesthetic switch" / Golden Collection). Norstein's two films and Hen, His Wife were moved in from C14-5.
  - C14-1 is re-sectioned by the studios of the **Golden Age of American animation**: Disney, Fleischer, Warner, MGM, UPA and silent pioneers. About 110 canonical shorts were added (Avery, Clampett, Jones, Fleischer Superman).
  - C12-14 Chinese Animation is re-sectioned (Shanghai Animation Film Studio classics / contemporary) and filled.
  - **Animated documentary** (NEW1) was missing entirely. Waltz with Bashir, Flee, They Shot the Piano Player and Piece by Piece were sitting in C12-5 (CG family film); Lusitania was in C14-1.
- **Canon fills (from TSPDT, mostly):**
  - Eastern Bloc schools: Zagreb (Satiemania, Dnevnik), Polish (Lenica, Borowczyk, Rybczyński), Hungarian (Rofusz, Jankovics, János vitéz) and Czech (Švankmajer, Trnka).
  - NFB classics; Quays and Reiniger; absolute film and visual music (Richter, Eggeling, Lye, Fischinger).
  - Arthouse features (Laguionie, Plympton, Sita Sings the Blues) and Fleischer features.
- **New sections:** C12-2 Fleischer features; C12-18 Italian auteur (Bozzetto) and East Asian independent (Yeon Sang-ho, McDull, On Happiness Road); C14-6 Japanese puppet animation (Kawamoto).

## Out of scope or treated as filters
- Hentai and Urotsukidōji are explicit, so out of scope. Web animation and ONA are out of scope.
- "Late-night anime" and "Shōwa-era TV anime" are broadcast slots or eras spanning every genre. They are left to the decade filter, not made into leaves; their titles are already in their genre leaves.
- The OVA boom is handled as era sections, not a leaf, because its titles are already well homed in cyberpunk, real-robot and similar leaves.

- The Soviet leaf includes Kievnauchfilm and Uzbekfilm shorts. Tallinnfilm (Estonian) stays in C14-5.
