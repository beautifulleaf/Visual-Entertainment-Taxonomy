Restructure R3 (November 2026)

Your review asked for a populated Design family; fuller, organised Visual Art leaves; Film & Television regrouped into Film, Television and Animation with the retired Asian Drama family gone; and leaves at the granularity of the Culinary Taxonomy instead of catch-alls such as a 630-title "Comedy Film". The requests added during the work are included below.

Film & Television — 962 leaves, 58,835 titles

  • Families: A Film (530 leaves, 37,750 films: movements, genres, A21 Short Film), B Television (268 leaves, 13,010 titles: series, B12 serial traditions, B13–B15 unscripted formats, B16 Live Event & Sports Television), C Animation (164 leaves, 8,075 titles: anime C1–C11 and non-Japanese features, series and shorts). Old codes map to new ones in taxonomy/r3/ft_migration.csv; retired leaves are no longer shown.
  • Granularity: median leaf 44 titles, none over 230; every leaf over 40 titles is divided into sections along a stated axis. No catch-all remains: the old "Comedy Film" is now dozens of comic engines and cycles (Zoolander is in Parody & Spoof).
  • Fundamentals: named era- and country-bounded cycles are leaves (Pre-Code Hollywood, Brat Pack, commedia sexy all'italiana, Ozploitation, Category III, Heimatfilm, Golden Age of Hindi Cinema ...); genres the field divides by era, nation or auteur are sectioned that way (giallo by director, kaiju by era, noir by national noir, sitcoms by US and UK eras). The page has country, decade and director filters.
  • Every subgenre represented: coverage passes against IMDb's genre interests, AllMovie, Wikipedia genre lists and TSPDT added 35 subgenres and 1,520 titles (Pink Film: 0 → 63, incl. the Roman Porno Reboot and Antiporno).
  • TSPDT Starting Lists: every film on the Starting List and the 21st Century Starting List now has a home — 20,770 placed (rules by director first, then passes), 4 restored after review; 64 are out of scope (games, web videos, podcasts, non-films, and explicit adult-trade films — see the Pleasure seam). Leaves that grew past 200 were split into 64 attested sub-leaves (e.g. Avant-Garde Short → Lyrical, Structural, Found-Footage, Landscape, Video Art) or re-sectioned.
  • B16 Live Event & Sports Television is new; web series and other internet-native content are out of scope (future Interactive Entertainment Taxonomy); news is out of scope.

Visual Art — 1,782 leaves, 48,702 kinds

  • Completeness audit against the gathered museum records: 19 traditions held in museum collections but missing from the tree are new leaves (among them Ancient Cypriot Pottery and Sculpture, Seljuk Stucco, Etruscan, Greek and Roman bronzes and metalwork, Byzantine Silver, Pala Painting, Classical Javanese Sculpture, Turkmen Jewellery, Kwoma Bark Painting); 12,887 objects filed only under a culture now count for the leaf their own title names (kris, netsuke, kimono, tsuba, haniwa…), adding holding institutions to 148 leaves. Stale cross-reference codes are corrected.
  • N Design (new, 34 leaves): furniture and lighting, product and industrial, and graphic design schools with their designed objects and designers; a new H7 Haute Couture & Fashion Design leaf.
  • Where to see it, as the organisation of non-artist traditions: per your direction, per-object lists were removed; each tradition leaf now leads with its schools, sub-types and centres, then the View all line (subject corpora, national aggregators, central databases) and Holding institutions: computed from 1.24 million gathered museum records mapped label by label, ranked by holdings, each with a link to the institution's search (668 leaves). Architecture and art in place keep only UNESCO and nationally designated sites, with the registers as View all.
  • Missing traditions found by the cross-reference: 91 new leaves (Tibetan and Coptic manuscript illumination, Ehon, Flatstyle, Ostionoid pottery, Noh masks, Northwest Coast woodcarving ...); 4 merged into existing leaves.
  • Catch-all artist buckets broken up: ~3,200 artists moved to their movements; 73 new movement and school leaves. Modern Art and Contemporary Art remain as residual leaves, grouped by named local group and then by country. One home per maker (726 cross-references).
