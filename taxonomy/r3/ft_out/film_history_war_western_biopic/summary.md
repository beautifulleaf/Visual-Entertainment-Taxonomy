## Merges and moves of old cycles
- Old A7-10 Partisan Film merged into A9-5 Partisan & Resistance Film. The Yugoslav cycle is section 1; Come and See, The Ascent and Defiance are in section 2.
- Old A7-4 Historical Drama (278) is gone. Its titles were split into Political Drama, Social Problem Film, Docudrama, Colonial & Slavery Film, Historical Epic, Medieval Film and Royal Court Drama by the mode of the film.
- Old A7-8 Biographical Film: films about one event moved to A8-13 Docudrama (e.g. Hotel Rwanda, Sully, Erin Brockovich, Changeling). Holocaust-rescue and resistance lives moved to A9 (One Life, Resistance, Sophie Scholl, Flame & Citron). 12 Years a Slave went to A8-7 and Elvira Madigan to A8-9.
- Lives of Jesus and the saints moved from Sword-and-Sandal to A11-4 Religious Biopic. Old Testament epics stay in A8-2.
- Shakespeare adaptations were gathered from Costume Drama, War Film and Biopic (Henry V, Richard III) into A8-10.
- Kagemusha and Ran moved from War Film to Jidaigeki.
- The small named cycles are kept as their own leaves: Atomic Bomb Film (1), Ostern (3), Acid Western (4), Singing Cowboy (2), Musical Western (4).
