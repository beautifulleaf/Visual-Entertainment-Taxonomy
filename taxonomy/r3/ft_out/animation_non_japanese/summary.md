## Judgement calls
- Old technique leaves (hand-drawn / CG) dissolved into canons and modes; stop-motion kept as a technique leaf in features and shorts, rotoscope and performance capture split out.
- Old "Adult Animated Feature" split: Bakshi/underground and adult comedy/action stay in C12-19; adult auteur films go to C12-18; stop-motion adult films (Anomalisa, Mad God, Mary and Max) to C12-16; adult TV-series films (South Park, Beavis) to C12-9.
- Network canons (Disney Afternoon, Nicktoons, Cartoon Cartoons, Disney Television Animation) hold comedies only; their action/superhero shows go to genre leaves (Avatar to C13-13, Teen Titans to C13-11).
- Rankin/Bass and other TV specials listed as features (Rudolph, Santa Claus Is Comin' to Town) moved to C14-7.
- Don't Hug Me I'm Scared and Skibidi Toilet were still in the input; placed in C14-6 sec 5 and C13-4.

## Weakly attested labels
"International Family Animated Feature", "Computer-Animated Family Film", "Adult Animated Action & Drama", "Studio Short Film" and "Toy-Based Film/Animated Series" put attested terms together rather than naming one established category. "Disney Television Animation" is a studio-canon leaf, in the same way as Nicktoons.
