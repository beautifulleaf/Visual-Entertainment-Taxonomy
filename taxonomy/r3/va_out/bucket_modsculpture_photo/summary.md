# bucket_modsculpture_photo — summary

Scope: D5-118 Modern Sculpture (1,600), J1-13 Contemporary (931), J1-12 Documentary (654), J1-9 Straight (512), J1-7 Early (400), J1-8 Pictorialism (330) — 4,427 maker rows, all artist-led.

## What changed
- Every maker placed exactly once: 3,810 rows stay in the six leaves (now sectioned), 94 go to 4 new leaves, 307 are moved out to 34 existing leaves.
- Each leaf has an axis, a better defn, and 4–5 "View all" catalogues (LoC Daguerreotype, Civil War and FSA/OWI collections, Talbot Catalogue Raisonné, Mapping Sculpture 1851–1951, Smithsonian Inventory of American Sculpture, Henry Moore catalogue, CCP Arizona, George Eastman Museum, Magnum archive, Met/MoMA/Getty/Tate/V&A searches).
- Named sections inside leaves come first. Early: inventors, daguerreotypists, British calotypists, French paper-negative/Mission héliographique, Civil War, Western surveys, chronophotography, Near East, British India, Yokohama shashin. Pictorialism: Linked Ring, Photo-Club de Paris, Vienna Trifolium, Kunstphotographie, Clarence H. White School, West Coast, geijutsu shashin. Straight: New York modernists, New Objectivity, Shinkō shashin, Hollywood glamour, Mexico. Documentary: early social documentary, FSA/OWI, Kamoinge, DRUM, Vivo/Provoke, Tio fotografer, AFAL, Lithuanian school, African studio, Fotoform, Generative photography. Contemporary: New Topographics, Pictures Generation, Vancouver/Boston/Helsinki Schools, YBA, staged photography. Sculpture: Rodin's assistants, Bande à Schnegg, direct carving, American Renaissance, Western sculptors, later New Sculpture, Vienna Secession/Wiener Werkstätte, Modernisme/Noucentisme, Tinerimea artistică, Bauhaus, Geometry of Fear, Park Place Group, New Generation, assemblage, Shona sculpture, Balinese carving.
- The rest are grouped by country of activity (normalised from Wikidata states; countries with <3 artists merged by region; groups >100 split by birth cohort). Rows got a short gloss (medium, countries, birth year).

## Doubts / not done
- An input row with an empty name (b. 1927, US) appears in 4 leaves. The validator forbids nameless kinds, so it is moved to A5-111; it should be deleted downstream.
- The buckets are really birth cohorts, so most of their artists are not pictorialist, straight or documentary in the strict sense. Defns now say this. Residual groups are by country.
- Fashion designers (Westwood, McQueen, Yamamoto, Lagerfeld, Lutens) and designers/architects in the sculpture bucket (Wirkkala, Sarpaneva, Mangiarotti, Pesce, Bogdanović) stay in country groups. The Design tab could take them later.
- Robert Adams (b. 1917, UK) in the Documentary input is the sculptor. He was moved to Modern Sculpture (Geometry of Fear).
- Magnum, Photo-Secession and f/64 founders already held elsewhere (Capa, Cartier-Bresson, Steichen, Brett Weston) were not duplicated into the new leaves.
- Joins by decade for Magnum are approximate, so sections are by birth generation instead.
