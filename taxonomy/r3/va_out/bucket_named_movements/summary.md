# bucket_named_movements: summary

27 named-movement leaves, now 4,343 kinds. No new leaves. 20 makers moved out, 22 rows dropped. Validator exits 0.

## What changed
- **Sections everywhere.** The flat "Artists" lists are now grouped by the schools, circles and phases the literature uses, each leaf with an axis line. Groups are in affinity order and rows within a group by birth year. Examples:
  - **Baroque:** Caravaggisti, Bolognese, Roman, Bamboccianti, Neapolitan, Genoese, Venetian, Florence/Siena, Lombard, Spanish Golden Age, colonial schools, Lorraine, French classicism, Peintres de la réalité, Académie royale, Régence, plus the Flemish, Dutch, German, Central European, Stuart, Scandinavian and others.
  - **Dutch Golden Age:** Haarlem, Pre-Rembrandtists, Rembrandt's pupils, Leiden fijnschilders, Delft, Utrecht, Dordrecht, Italianates, then landscape, marine, genre, still life and architecture.
  - **Northern Renaissance:** Cologne, Upper Rhine, Nuremberg, Little Masters, Danube school, Cranach, Avignon, Romanists, the Bruegel tradition and others.
  - **Impressionism:** Batignolles group, Crozant and Rouen schools, the Ten, Giverny, Boston, Old Lyme, Hoosier, California, Heidelberg, NEAC, Secessions, Luminism and Skagen.
  - **Expressionism:** Brücke, NKVM and Blaue Reiter, Sturm and Pathetiker, Dresden and Hamburg Secessions, Lost Generation, Latem, Bergen School and De Ploeg, Rot-Blau.
  - **Chinese and Japanese leaves:** by dynasty, lineage and exhibiting body.
- **Glosses.** The machine glosses ("movement X", "country group") are replaced with "Painter, b. YEAR · country", and sub-type and school rows got real glosses. Possession links are kept as "· Possession: X (B1)", with the URL in the source field.
- **Catalogues ("View all").** Every leaf has 3–4 links: WGA, Fondazione Zeri, RKD, the Rembrandt Database, Rubenianum, the Cranach Digital Archive, the Van Gogh Museum and its letters edition, the Cézanne catalogue, Birmingham's Pre-Raphaelite resource, the Rossetti Archive, the Warhol collection, the Dalí catalogue raisonné, the Brücke-Museum, the FU Berlin "Degenerate Art" database, ColBase, Bunka Isan Online, MOMAT, the National Palace Museum and Palace Museum archives, and Met, V&A, BM, AIC, Tate and Smithsonian searches.
- **Mughal Painting (the only tradition-led leaf).**
  - Works: 12 named manuscripts and albums with Wikipedia articles, grouped by reign. The rule is in the axis. They are listed from knowledge, because the gathered manuscript file has no named Mughal manuscripts.
  - Collections: computed from the regional object files (V&A 2909, Cleveland 1224, Freer|Sackler 90, Volkenkunde 40, AIC 16, Aga Khan 12).
  - Makers: split by atelier and reign.
- **Renaissance Sculpture.** The two "Padua" museum culture labels are merged into a new sub-type row, "Paduan small bronzes".

## Moves
- **Within the pass:**
  - Master of Estopiñán and Francesc Comes: Baroque → Gothic Painting.
  - Cesare da Sesto: Northern Renaissance → Italian Renaissance.
  - Figino: Northern Renaissance → Mannerism.
  - Schmidt-Rottluff and Lasar Segall: Impressionism → Expressionism.
  - Su Shi, Mi Fu, Mi Youren, Wen Tong, Li Gonglin, Wang Tingyun and Wang Wei: Gongbi → Literati.
  - Wang Xizhi, Wang Xianzhi and Huang Tingjian: Gongbi → Chinese Calligraphy.
- **Out of the pass:**
  - Gutenberg, Jenson, Griffo, Caslon, Bodoni, Walbaum and the Amoretti: → C1-12 Type Design.
  - Kanoldt, Schrimpf, Räderscheidt, Grossberg, Schlichter and Georg Scholz: → A5-68 New Objectivity.
  - Hasui, Koson, Kōitsu, Takahashi Shōtei and Yamamura Kōka: → B2-18 Shin-hanga.
  - Maekawa Senpan: → B2-19 Sōsaku-hanga.
  - Kim Saeng: → C1-13 Korean Calligraphy.

## Dropped
- The 13 blank-name junk rows.
- Self-label evidence rows (Yōga, Nihonga, Mughal painting; the last is kept as an alias).
- Duplicate rows: Awadh (Lucknow), Miskina, Faizullah.
- The two Padua culture labels.

## Doubts
- Some rows sit in the wrong leaf but have nowhere better to go, so they are kept in "outliers" or "others" groups: Maria Cunitz, Christina of Sweden and Esther Inglis (Baroque); Carême, Bianconi, Minton and Roubo (Old Master Print); Araniko and Lam Qua (Literati); Tarō Okamoto (Nihonga); Simon Kozhin, b. 1979 (Impressionism); Laura Alma-Tadema (Dutch Golden Age).
- Two rows have a wrongly matched birth year. "Richard Wilson" in Rococo (b. 1953) is kept as the 18th-century painter. "François Dumont" (b. 1751) is in Baroque Sculpture.
- Some group memberships are broad-brush: the Rose + Croix group in Symbolism, the Taishō societies in Yōga, and Bunten versus Inten in Nihonga.
- Two Mughal album articles could not be fetched to confirm: Minto Album and St. Petersburg Muraqqa.
