- Taiyōzoku Film (1, Crazed Fruit) is merged into A16-15 Juvenile Delinquency Film, which names the cycle in its definition and aliases.
- Old Sports Film is split into six attested leaves for single sports plus a residual leaf, A16-29, which is sectioned by sport.
- Old Road Movie (202) is split into A16-36 Road Movie and A16-37 Buddy Film.
- Shomingeki, Christian Film and Banlieue Film are kept as they were.
- A Hood Film leaf was considered and dropped: its few candidates went to Social Realism or Social Problem Film.

## Weakly attested labels
- "Parent–Child Drama" is a descriptive term. Its sources are the Wikipedia categories for father–son and mother–daughter films.
- "Teacher Film" and "Campus Film" are sourced to Category:Films about educators and to Campus novel.
- "Juvenile Delinquency Film" (JD film) is a trade term without its own Wikipedia article. A16-15 also holds a "Teens in Crisis" section.
- "Banlieue Film" (cinéma de banlieue) has no English Wikipedia article. Its sources are La Haine and Banlieue.

## Routes
None. The road comedies (Dumb and Dumber, EuroTrip, Vacation) and the sports comedies stay in this block. They are hybrids rather than misplacements.
