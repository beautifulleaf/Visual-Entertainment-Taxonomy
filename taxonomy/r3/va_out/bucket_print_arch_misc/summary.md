# bucket_print_arch_misc (R3 VA pass) — summary (saved by coordinator from the pass report)

21 leaves, 1 new (Franco-Belgian Comics, B1, 58 artists from B1-5), 304 makers moved out (A5-111 85, I3-19 40, B1-3 33, I3-20 27, E1-26 13, I3-17 11, K6-19 9, B2-9 7 ...), 5,131 kinds; ~2,230 rows added (~2,020 rule-based works-catalogue rows from Wikidata, ~100 canonical makers/works from knowledge). Every leaf has catalogues; none has ≤ 4 kinds.

- Architecture (11 style leaves): works rule = every building in architecture_exemplars.csv tagged with the style or a listed sub-style with sitelinks ≥ 1 or a heritage/UNESCO listing (pre-modern styles with an upper date cut-off); sections by sub-style/phase, generic-tagged buildings by building type; glosses country, date, architect, UNESCO/heritage. Pre-modern architects by region of their buildings; restorers and revival architects last. K6-22/K6-26 architects by named school (Metabolism, De Stijl, Neues Bauen, Como Rationalism, Neo-Liberty, Grup R, Escuela de Madrid, Porto and Ticino Schools, Radical architecture, Yugoslav and Soviet Modernism, critical regionalism, SuperDutch, paper architecture), else country; high-tech → K6-19, deconstructivists → K6-25, postmodernists → K6-24, New Classical → K6-20; designers → I3-19/I3-20, photographers → J1-13, artists → A5-111 / M1.
- Illustration B1-5: by type of illustration, then national comics traditions; American comics → B1-3, Marcinelle → B1-8, manga → B1-9, posters → B2-9, ~70 gallery artists → A5-111. B1-6, B2-14, B2-15 by school, venue and trade.
- Art Deco / Art Nouveau: by medium or school; Bauhaus makers → I3-17; 14 Art Nouveau rows that are Art Deco → I3-16.
- Black-figure / red-figure: painters by Beazley's workshops and phases (Pioneer Group, Mannerists, Leagros Group ...), South Italian by fabric; 37 red-figure painters E1-27 → E1-28, 3 back; Corinthian → E1-26, Laconian → E1-29, Etruscan → E1-34; named vases added.
- Persian Carpet: ~70 weaving centres in 7 regional groups plus design types; works = 6 carpets with Wikipedia articles + 19 titled Iranian carpets from islamic_objects.csv; Collections top 8 (V&A 254 of the gathered carpets).
- Gothic Book Illustration: works = named manuscripts with sitelinks ≥ 1 + 22 canonical ones from knowledge (gathered data leans to US libraries via Digital Scriptorium); Collections 31 institutions with ≥ 10 items; Mozarabic, Carolingian and Renaissance illuminators → C2-23, C2-20, C2-25.
- Doubts: "Other named Attic hands" holds painters not confidently placed in a workshop; odd Wikidata style tags (e.g. Hexham Abbey as Romano-Gothic) kept by the uniform rule; no Collections for vases and Art Deco/Nouveau (no gathered objects) — catalogue links instead.
