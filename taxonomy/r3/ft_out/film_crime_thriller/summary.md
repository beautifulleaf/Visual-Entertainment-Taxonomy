## Merges and splits
- The old catch-alls are split by plot engine. Crime Drama (352) went to Social Problem Film, True-Crime, Drug, Hitman, White-Collar Crime, Gambling, Con-Artist and Lovers-on-the-Run. Police & Detective (214) went to Police Procedural, Police Film, Private-Eye, Nordic Noir and Serial-Killer. Suspense Thriller (247) went to Kidnapping, Hostage & Siege, Home-Invasion, Real-Time, Wrong-Man and Survival. Psychological Thriller (195) is split into Psychological and Domestic. Mystery (101) is split into Whodunit, Inverted Detective Story and Mystery Film. Political & Conspiracy is split in two. Spy (172) is now Spy Film plus Spy Comedy.
- Film Noir & Neo-Noir is split by period. Classical noir (to c.1960) includes the French noir and the Nikkatsu-era Japanese noir as sections. Melville's films from 1962 on go to Neo-Noir's Professional-Criminal section.
- Old Survival & Death-Game is split into Survival Thriller and Death Game Film.
- Every old small cycle is kept as its own leaf: Yakuza, Blaxploitation, Hood, Cine Quinqui (1 title), Pinky Violence (2), Poliziottesco (2), Giallo.
- New small attested cycles: Mumbai Noir (3) and Krimi (1, Face of the Frog). Both are named cycles that the field recognises.
- Buddy-cop titles were too few to stand alone, so they sit in A2-16 Police Film. Mabuse and Les Vampires sit in the Classical Gangster Cycle.
## Weakly attested labels
- Gambling Film, Lovers-on-the-Run Film and Con-Artist Film are common critical terms but have no Wikipedia genre article; they are sourced via Gambling, Bonnie and Clyde, Road movie and Confidence trick.
- Wrong-Man Thriller is sourced via Hitchcockian. Serial-Killer Thriller is sourced via Serial killer and Crime thriller.
- Social Problem Film section 4, Desperation Crime Drama (62), is the loosest section in the block.
## Routes
- None. Hybrids carry see_also instead, e.g. Spy Kids sits in Spy Comedy and Blacula in Blaxploitation.
